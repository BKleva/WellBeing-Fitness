75-Minute Sound Bath Step away from the noise of daily life and immerse yourself in 75 minutes of deep rest, relaxation, and restoration. During this guided sound bath, you'll be invited to settle comfortably on your mat while soothing vibrations from crystal singing bowls, drums, chimes, and other therapeutic instruments gently wash over you. These resonant frequencies are designed to help quiet the mind, calm the nervous system, and create space for profound relaxation. No experience is necessary, simply come as you are. This is a meditative practice where there are no poses to master or expectations to meet. Many participants leave feeling lighter, more grounded, and deeply refreshed. Whether you're looking to reduce stress, improve sleep, reconnect with yourself, or simply enjoy a peaceful pause, this experience offers an opportunity to slow down and nourish your mind, body, and spirit. Please bring: A yoga mat, blanket, and water, and come dressed in comfortable clothing that helps you feel relaxed, cozy, and at ease. Give yourself permission to rest. Your nervous system will thank you.
