Women's Wellness: common questions

Who leads the women's wellness programs?

Melissa Matheson, a Master Health, Wellness and Menopause Specialist and Certified Menopause Coaching Specialist. Physical therapist Krista Simon is also a certified menopause coaching specialist.

What symptoms can coaching help with?

Our programs support brain fog, hot flushes, weight gain, sleep, energy levels and the other common symptoms of midlife changes.
