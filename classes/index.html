Class styles

Find the practice that fits you.

Every class offers modifications, so you can meet yourself where you are.

Slow Flow

Beginner friendly

Poses are held longer to build muscular endurance, with conscious breathing and meditative movement to release tension and clear the mind.

Gentle Yoga

Beginner friendly

A soothing practice of stretching and relaxation at a slow, deliberate pace, with modifications offered throughout.

All Levels

All levels

A creative, breath-linked flow that increases flexibility and tones muscle. Props and variations let you meet yourself where you are.

Hatha

Some experience helpful

A strong, dynamic practice that builds strength of body and mind and leaves you invigorated.

Vinyasa

Some experience helpful

Breath and movement combine in a flowing sequence of poses, building strength and flexibility.

Yin

All levels

Seated and reclined postures held two to five minutes let joints and fascia release. A meditative way to reduce stress and anxiety.

Restorative

Beginner friendly

Fully supported by props in a candlelit room, with hands-on assistance and gentle massage. No experience necessary.

Mindful Flow

All levels

Poses held for multiple breaths to release the legs, hips, back and shoulders while working muscles throughout the body.

Kundalini

All levels

A 60-minute morning practice of kriyas, breathwork, flow and mantra to move energy through the body.

Yoga Sculpt

Active

Upbeat cardio, yoga poses and hand weights to build lean muscle and boost metabolism.

Teen Yoga

Ages 11–19

A slow-paced, confidence-building practice of strength, flexibility and conscious breathing for teens.

Pilates Mat-1

Beginner friendly

A foundational series for whole-body strength and a stable core: better posture, balance, flexibility and coordination.

Pilates with Weights

All levels

Pilates and standing balance work with 2–5 lb hand weights or weighted balls for toning and core stability.

Yoga Pilates Fusion

All levels

Pilates stability for the shoulders, core and pelvis, paced like a yoga class with standing and balance poses.

Barre Fitness

All levels

A low-impact, full-body workout blending ballet, Pilates, yoga and strength training with precise, repetitive movements.

Barre Cardio Fusion

Active

Low-impact cardio, light-weight sculpting, barre work for leg toning, core work and stretching.

Your first visit

Come as you are.

  • What to bring: a mat and water bottle if you like. We have mats, water and all the props you need: blankets, blocks and more.
  • What to wear: anything that feels comfortable to move and relax in.
  • Where to put things: cubbies and hangers in the studio lobby for belongings and shoes.
  • Who can join: weekly classes are for ages 15+, with Teen and Tween programs for ages 11+.

FAQ

Your first visit, covered

How do I register for group classes?

Sign up on the live class schedule. We have two locations, Groton and Westford, plus online class options. Walk-ins are welcome as long as there is an open spot, but please always check the schedule before heading to the studio to be sure the class is running.

When should I arrive?

Arrive at least five minutes before the start of class or session. Classes begin on time, and arriving late may result in a missed class and the loss of a class from your class count.

What if I need to cancel a group class?

Class registrations can be canceled by logging into your account up to two hours before class time. If you can't attend and don't cancel, you'll be charged for the spot.
