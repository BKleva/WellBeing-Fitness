FAQ

Private Pilates: common questions

Do I need experience to try Pilates?

No. Exercises adapt to every fitness level, so beginners and experienced participants are equally welcome.

What's the difference between reformer and mat Pilates?

Mat Pilates uses your body weight on the floor. Reformer work uses a spring-resistance machine that adds support and challenge. Private sessions can use either or blend the two.
